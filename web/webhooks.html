Each delivery is a JSON POST with X-FinBlade-Event, X-FinBlade-Delivery and X-FinBlade-Signature: t=<epoch>,v1=<hex> where hex = HMAC-SHA256(secret, "<t>.<body>"). Verify the signature and reject t older than 5 minutes. Answer 2xx; a 5xx or timeout is retried for ~3 hours (5s, 30s, 2m, 5m, 15m, 30m, 1h, 1h), a 4xx is final. Retries resend the identical body under the same delivery id — deduplicate on it. Full spec: docs/WEBHOOKS.md.
